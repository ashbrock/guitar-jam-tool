# Virtuosity Drums (subset)

- **Instrument:** Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios, https://versilian-studios.com/virtuosity-drums/. Played by Austin McMahon on the house kit at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston.
- **Source:** https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums.
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), see `LICENSE.txt`. Credit isn't required, but we give it.
- **What we changed:** we kept kick (snares off), snare centre hits and the closed hi-hat: 4–6 velocity layers each, with 2 round robins for kick and hat. For each hit, the kick mic, snare mic and overheads are summed (like the library's "basic kit"). Hits are trimmed (kick and snare 1.6 s, hat 0.9 s) and encoded as AAC (112 kbps, stereo). Built with `scripts/prepare-bass-drums.mjs`.
