# Wurlitzer EP200 samples

**Author:** Greg Sullivan (http://www.sullivang.net/)
**Instrument:** Wurlitzer EP200 Electric Piano, v1.1 (16 May 1999)
**SFZ mapping:** kinwie, via SFZ Instruments (https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos)
**Files:** AAC (.m4a) conversions from the smpldsnds mirror (https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-greg-sullivan-e-pianos). Five files in that mirror were corrupt (`ab3f`, `db4f`, `f2f`, `g4ff`, `g4pp`); those were re-encoded from the original FLAC files in the SFZ Instruments repository (AAC-LC, 80 kbps, mono).
