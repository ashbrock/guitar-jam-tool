# Swagbass

- **Instrument:** Swagbass by Karoryfer Samples (D. Smolken), https://karoryfer.com/. An Ibanez BTB-400QM bass with old flatwound strings, neck pickup, recorded direct.
- **Source:** https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swagbass ("clean" mapping).
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), see `LICENSE.txt`. Credit isn't required, but we give it.
- **What we changed:** we kept 9 notes (A1–A3, a minor third apart), 3 dynamics and 2 round robins. Each is trimmed to 3 s with a 0.5 s fade and encoded as AAC (80 kbps, mono). Built with `scripts/prepare-bass-drums.mjs`.
